# Credit reports and scores

Your credit reports and scores have an impact on your finances. Our resources can help you better understand them, learn how to correct errors, and improve your credit record over time.

## How to avoid credit repair scams

Improving your credit takes time, and no company can legally remove accurate, negative information from your credit report. Learn how credit repair really works, how to spot scams, and where to find trustworthy help so you can rebuild your credit with confidence.
